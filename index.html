About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in cryptography, at the intersection between theory and practice. My research interests include implementation security, side-channel attacks and prevention, post-quantum cryptography, and constructive cryptography, with focus on composability and modular design.

I also enjoy writing software for computers and embedded devices, using best practices in software engineering and keeping my code simple, modular, efficient, and easy to maintain and to improve. I have experience with many programming languages, including Assembly Languages (x86, ARM, AVR), Rust, C++, C, Python, and with microcontroller programming, including ARM Cortex and Atmel ATMega.
